About me

Business first.
Then message, language and visuals.

Before working on communication, brand and content, I worked inside B2B companies.
That changed the way I look at communication: I don’t start from how it should appear, but from what it needs to clarify, achieve or enable for the business.

I’m Gioia Mongelli, a Brand Communication & Design Strategist.
My work sits at the intersection of communication, B2B commercial experience and design. For years, I worked closely with clients, operational priorities, commercial materials, presentations and messages that had to work in real business situations, not just look correct on paper.
Today I work with B2B startups and companies whose market-facing communication does not give clients, partners or investors a clear enough reason to give them time, trust or budget.
This can happen for different reasons: the message changes too much across different audiences, materials are created before it is clear what they need to communicate, or language and visuals do not reflect the company’s real level, to name just a few.

The work consists in understanding where that distance starts and acting on what needs to change: message, positioning, language, content, commercial materials and design.
The goal is simple: to make sure what the company shows, says and presents helps the right people understand why it is worth listening, trusting and moving forward.
