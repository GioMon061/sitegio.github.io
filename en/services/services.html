Fractional Head of Brand & Strategy

If every conversation still needs too much explaining and that is costing you time, trust and opportunities, start here.

Fractional Head of Brand & Strategy is an ongoing collaboration for B2B companies that can no longer afford every opportunity to depend on a live explanation.
It is built for situations where the product has value, but too much work still has to happen before clients, partners or investors get to the real point: rebuilding trust, adding context, correcting confusion, explaining why it is worth going deeper.
The work brings messaging, visual identity, LinkedIn, website, deck and commercial materials under one direction, so people come to the conversation better prepared: they understand sooner, trust sooner, ask better questions and have more to work with when deciding whether to move forward.

Social Master

If prospects look you up on LinkedIn and still come away unsure why they should trust you, start here.

Social Master is an ongoing collaboration for B2B companies that want to use LinkedIn to build trust before direct contact, not just to publish content.
It is built for situations where the profile, company page, content, visuals and activity are not doing enough yet: people land on LinkedIn, find weak or scattered signals, and the conversation still starts cold.
The work builds a stronger and more intentional presence: clearer profiles and company page, written and visual content with direction, criteria for what to publish, who to engage with and how to open useful conversations.
So LinkedIn does not stay a passive shop window: it prepares clients, partners or investors better before they speak to you.

Founder Communication Strategy

If you are communicating by trial and error and need to know what to fix first, start here.

If your LinkedIn doesn’t reflect your business value, you lose credibility quietly. Founder Communication Strategy Sessions, is a paid consultation made of two 60-minute sessions.
It is built for situations where your profile, messaging, content or DMs are not helping enough, but you do not need generic advice or an endless list of things to fix.
Across two calls, we look at how your business is currently being explained, where confusion starts, what is costing you credibility, and what should be fixed first.
You leave with a written recap, a clear direction and 3–4 concrete moves to apply over the following weeks.

Marketing Design Pro

If the direction is already there, but decks, PDFs and materials make the work look less clear or less solid, start here.

Marketing Design Pro is for B2B companies that already have content, ideas or a clear direction, but lose strength in the way those things are presented.
It is built for situations where decks, reports, one-pagers, brochures or commercial materials feel heavy, hard to read or less professional than the business they represent.
The work turns existing content into clearer, better organised and more credible materials, so the person receiving them understands faster, follows with less effort and takes the company more seriously.

Full Brand

If the company is solid, but the website, materials and visual identity still make it look too early, start here.

Full Brand is a service for B2B companies that work better than they look.
It is built for situations where the issue is not “needing a better logo”, but the fact that the website, materials, deck and visual identity do not yet show how serious, solid and ready the company actually is.
The work builds a more credible visual and communication foundation: identity, guidelines and key materials. So people meeting the company for the first time do not have to get past a weak first impression before taking it seriously.
